VG-project privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

What VG-project is

VG-project is a private tool used only by the owner of the Benchtop Physics YouTube channel to publish that channel's own videos and to read that channel's own performance data. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information accessed

  • With your permission (Google sign-in), VG-project accesses the YouTube channel you authorize: its videos, playlists, comments on its videos, and its YouTube Analytics reports.
  • It does not access other Google services, and it does not collect information from the channel's viewers beyond what YouTube API Services return for the channel's own videos (for example, public comments).

How information is used

  • To upload and manage the channel's own videos.
  • To measure how those videos perform, so that future videos can be improved.
  • Information is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties.

Storage and retention

  • The sign-in token and data retrieved from YouTube API Services are stored in the owner's private Amazon Web Services account, encrypted at rest, and accessible only to the owner.
  • Data obtained from YouTube API Services is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. Aggregated statistics derived from it may be kept.
  • VG-project does not use cookies or tracking on this site.

Revoking access

You can revoke VG-project's access at any time on your Google Account's third-party access page. To have stored data deleted, email the contact below.
